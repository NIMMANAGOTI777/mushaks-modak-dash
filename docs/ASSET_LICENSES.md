## 🕉️ Cultural Respect & Sensitivity Statement

In strict accordance with the contest guidelines:
- Lord Ganesha is respectfully depicted in the illuminated festive temple pandal background in traditional dignified iconography (Crown, Mukut, Vamamukhi trunk, Modak blessing).
- Lord Ganesha is **never** presented as an obstacle, combatant, or subject of harm.
- The gameplay celebrates the devotion and joyful spirit of Ganesh Chaturthi through traditional elements (Modak, Durva, Rangoli, Dhol Tasha, Marigold flowers, and Toran).
