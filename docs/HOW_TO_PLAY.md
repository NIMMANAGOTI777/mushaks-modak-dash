# 📖 How to Play - Mushak's Modak Dash

**Mushak's Modak Dash** is a fast-paced, festive 2.5D endless runner created by **Team BAPPA BYTES** for the **Ganesh Chaturthi Game Design Contest 2026**.

---

## 🎯 Objective
Guide Mushak (Lord Ganesha's loyal and agile divine vahana) through the illuminated, rangoli-adorned streets during Ganesh Chaturthi. Collect delicious Modaks, gather sacred Durva Grass to activate protective shields, leap over obstacles, duck under hanging torans, build combo multipliers, and achieve the highest festival score!

---

## 🎮 Controls

### 💻 Desktop / Laptop:
| Action | Primary Key | Alternate Key |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Move Left** | `A` | `Left Arrow (←)` |
| **Move Right** | `D` | `Right Arrow (→)` |
| **Jump** | `W` | `Up Arrow (↑)` or `Spacebar` |
| **Slide / Duck** | `S` | `Down Arrow (↓)` |
| **Pause / Resume** | `P` | `Escape (Esc)` |

### 📱 Mobile / Touch Devices:
- **Swipe Left**: Change to left lane
- **Swipe Right**: Change to right lane
- **Swipe Up**: Jump into the air
- **Swipe Down**: Slide / Duck under low obstacles
- **On-Screen D-Pad**: Touch navigation buttons placed comfortably at screen corners.

## ✨ Collectibles & Scoring

| Item | Texture / Appearance | Value | Special Effect |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Regular Modak** | Steamed White/Ivory Modak | **+10 pts** (Base) | Feeds combo counter |
| **Jumbo Modak** | Radiant Golden Shimmering Modak | **+50 pts** (Base) | Feeds combo counter + golden sparkle burst |
| **Durva Grass** | Tied bundle of sacred green blades | **Shield Item** | Activates **Durva Shield (5s)** |

$$\text{Total Score} = (\text{Distance} \times 1) + (\text{Regular Modaks} \times 10 \times \text{Multiplier}) + (\text{Jumbo Modaks} \times 50 \times \text{Multiplier})$$

---

## 🛡️ Durva Shield Mechanic
- Collecting **Durva Grass** grants Mushak a **5-second protective aura**.
- The shield completely absorbs the **first obstacle collision** during those 5 seconds without ending the run.
- When absorbed:
  - Shield shatters with a protective crystal sound.
  - Mushak gains brief invulnerability frames to recover safely.
  - The combo counter resets.
- If no collision occurs, the shield naturally expires after 5 seconds.

---

## 🔥 Combo Multiplier System
- Collect **3 consecutive Modaks** within 2 seconds $\rightarrow$ **x2 Multiplier**
- Collect **6 consecutive Modaks** within 2 seconds $\rightarrow$ **x3 Multiplier** 🔥
- The combo multiplier amplifies all Modak collectible points!
- The combo resets if more than 2 seconds elapse without collecting a Modak or upon collision.

## 🚧 Festival Obstacles

1. **Flower Cart**: Marigold-loaded wooden cart $\rightarrow$ *Jump over or switch lanes*.
2. **Stacked Crates**: Wooden festive storage crates $\rightarrow$ *Jump over or switch lanes*.
3. **Road Barrier**: Striped festival barricade $\rightarrow$ *Jump over or switch lanes*.
4. **Decorative Pot**: Painted terracotta Matka $\rightarrow$ *Switch lanes*.
5. **Hanging Toran / Garland**: Low hanging floral decorations $\rightarrow$ *Slide / Duck underneath*.

---

## 🏆 Difficulty Progression
- **0–30 seconds (EASY)**: Relaxed speed, generous obstacle spacing, plenty of modak trails.
- **30–60 seconds (MEDIUM)**: Moderate speed increase, dynamic obstacle variety.
- **60+ seconds (HARD)**: High-speed festivity, rapid reflexes required, multi-lane obstacle challenges!
