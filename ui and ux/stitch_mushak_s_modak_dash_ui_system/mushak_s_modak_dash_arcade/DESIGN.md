## Brand & Style

This design system establishes an energetic, festive, and hyper-tactile mobile-first arcade aesthetic for a celebratory endless runner. The emotional core balances reverence and unadulterated arcade fun: the warmth of late-night Ganesh Chaturthi street celebrations, sparkling diya lamps, fragrant marigolds, and the dynamic thrill of Mushak darting through bustling pandals.

The design movement combines **Tactile / Skeuomorphic Arcade** with **Festive Indian Maximalism**:
- Thick, pillowy 3D physical-style buttons with hard extrusion bevels and golden metallic rims that feel instantly pressable on touchscreens.
- Midnight festival atmospheres built through warm charcoal, roasted terracotta, and espresso tones lit from within by vermilion fire and luminous saffron auras.
- Sacred accents translated into responsive UI elements: durva grass indicators, brass filigree modal brackets, modak-shaped progress indicators, and radiant rangoli burst patterns for combo streaks.
- Crisp game feedback: high-contrast badges, glowing counters, and dynamic arcade physics animations.

## Colors

The palette reproduces the radiance of oil lamps, offerings, and brass idols set against a night sky.

### Base Palettes & Roles
- **Backdrop / Midnight Foundations**:
  - Night Abyss (`#120907`): Deepest canvas base for fullscreen gameplay overlays and main menus.
  - Pandal Shadows (`#1A0D08`): Middle-tier backdrop for nested panels and HUD backdrops.
  - Temple Teak (`#23120B`): Card backgrounds, list view backdrops, and inactive states.
- **Divine Fire & Saffron (Primary Engine)**:
  - Rich Saffron (`#FF7A00`): Dominant call-to-action color, button face gradients, and vital badges.
  - Vermilion Torch (`#FF5100`): Button depth/extrusion skirts, alert counters, high combo multipliers.
- **Golden Glow & Ornaments (Secondary Engine)**:
  - Temple Gold (`#FFC72C`): Score dials, star ratings, brass frames, coin/modak iconography.
  - Sacred Shimmer (`#FFE082`): Highlight edges, specular reflections on bevels, and golden rim strokes.
- **Durva & Prosperity (Tertiary Engine)**:
  - Sacred Durva (`#2E7D32`): Boost bars, revive indicators, health nodes, positive score deltas.
  - Sprout Green (`#4CAF50`): Active toggles, shield power-ups, success toasts.
- **Devotional Text & Accents**:
  - Warm Ivory (`#FFF8E7`): Primary typography, maximum readability against deep warm darkness.
  - Marigold Amber (`#FFA000`): Secondary label text, warning glows, active rank badges.

### Interaction & Feedback Colors
- **Coin/Modak Sparkle**: Linear gradient from `#FFE082` (10%) through `#FFC72C` (60%) to `#FF7A00` (100%).
- **Danger / Obstacle Impact**: Pulsing vermilion crimson (`#D32F2F` to `#FF5100`).

## Typography

The typography strategy pairs expressive, thick, curved display letterforms with high-legibility geometric sans for dynamic data and body copy.

- **Display & Headlines (Epilogue)**: Imparts the joyful, bulbous, heavyweight energy required for carnival-tier game titles, level clearances, "SUPER DASH" banners, and chunk-style CTA buttons. Custom heavy strokes echo festive Indian woodblock types and brass carvings.
- **Numbers & Arcade Readouts**: Epilogue 900-weight with tabular digits ensures score tickers and combo multipliers remain stable and do not jitter during rapid accumulation.
- **In-Game HUD & Body Text (Plus Jakarta Sans)**: Balanced, friendly, highly legible even against glowing particles or translucent dark backgrounds. Used for power-up descriptions, settings dialogs, and leaderboard positions.
- **Text Embellishments**:
  - Hero display text applies a double-drop outline: `text-shadow: 0 4px 0 #120907, 0 0 12px rgba(255, 122, 0, 0.6)`.
  - Golden subheadings apply a 1px vertical stroke highlight using `#FFE082`.

## Layout & Spacing

Mushak's Modak Dash uses an adaptive touch-first grid calibrated for portrait and landscape orientation with dynamic thumb zones.

### Safe Zones & Layout Architecture
- **In-Game HUD (Fixed No-Grid Anchor)**: Anchors along top and bottom safe-area insets. Modak balance and meter HUD sit pinned at `margin` from top edge. Touch d-pad/swipe action affordances occupy the bottom 25% height thumb cradle.
- **Menu & Shop Layouts (Fluid Responsive Grid)**:
  - Mobile Portrait: 4 columns, `gutter` of `0.75rem`, outer safe screen `margin` of `1rem`.
  - Tablet/Landscape: 8 to 12 columns, `gutter` of `1.25rem`, outer margin clamped to `2rem`.
- **Rhythm**:
  - `space-xs` (4px): Spacing between icon badges and ticker values.
  - `space-sm` (8px): Internal gap inside pill counters, compact stat tiles.
  - `space-md` (16px): Standard padding inside cards, item grids, modal wrappers.
  - `space-lg` (24px): Separation between game state cards, action clusters, modal CTAs.
  - `space-xl` (36px): Outer vertical rhythm separating modal header from hero reward pedestals.

## Elevation & Depth

Depth in this design system is physical and luminous, avoiding sterile flat surfaces in favor of warm brass, physical button extrusions, and oil-lamp glows.

### Elevation Levels
- **Level 0 (Backdrop Grounding)**: Solid `#120907` background with subtle radial vignette overlays (`radial-gradient(circle at 50% 30%, #23120B 0%, #120907 80%)`).
- **Level 1 (Subordinate Panels & Shop Slots)**: Surface `#1A0D08` with an inner shadow (`inset 0 2px 4px rgba(0,0,0,0.6)`) and a subtle 1.5px border colored in deep brass (`#4A2912`).
- **Level 2 (Modals & Pause Trays)**: Surface `#23120B` surrounded by a 2.5px solid border (`#FFC72C`), backed by an ambient festival bloom shadow: `0 12px 36px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.85), 0 0 20px rgba(255, 122, 0, 0.25)`.
- **Level 3 (Interactive 3D Game Buttons)**: Physical extruded layers:
  - Top face: Gradient from `#FF7A00` to `#FF5100`.
  - Top specular highlight: `inset 0 2px 0 rgba(255, 224, 130, 0.7)`.
  - Physical bottom bevel: `0 6px 0 #9A2C00` (drops to `0 1px 0 #9A2C00` when pressed).
  - Ambient floor shadow: `0 10px 18px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.5)`.
- **Level 4 (Floating Combos & Power-Up Orbs)**: Suspended items cast radial saffron-and-gold halos: `box-shadow: 0 0 24px #FFC72C, 0 0 48px rgba(255, 81, 0, 0.6)`.

## Shapes

The shape system blends friendly arcade geometry with sacred decorative silhouettes.

- **Base Radius (Rounded Level 2)**: 0.5rem (8px) for buttons, item slots, and chips. Generates a chunky, tactile appearance that resists fragile sharpness.
- **Large Radius (`rounded-lg`, 1rem / 16px)**: Applied to main dialog containers, reward chests, and stats containers.
- **Capsule / Pill Radius (`rounded-full`)**: Applied strictly to progress meters, health vials, modak counters, and small floating multiplier tags.
- **Filigree & Corner Accents**: Floating modal panels use 45-degree corner notches or brass brackets reminiscent of temple pandal woodwork. 
- **Rangoli Geometries**: Stamped background medallions and combo burst effects use 8-pointed star and scalloped petal geometry.

## Components

### 1. Chunky 3D Arcade Buttons
- **Primary ("PLAY", "BOOST", "CLAIM")**:
  - Height: Minimum 54px for finger tap-targets.
  - Surface: Linear gradient (`180deg, #FF7A00 0%, #FF5100 100%`).
  - Rim: 2px solid `#FFE082`.
  - Depth Bevel: `box-shadow: inset 0 2px 0 rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.35), 0 6px 0 #8B2500, 0 10px 12px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.45)`.
  - Text: `label-lg`, uppercase, `#FFF8E7`, with 1px black drop shadow.
  - Active/Pressed State: `transform: translateY(5px); box-shadow: inset 0 1px 0 rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.2), 0 1px 0 #8B2500`.
- **Secondary ("SHOP", "PAUSE", "SETTINGS")**:
  - Surface: `#23120B` with subtle `#3D1F12` vertical gradient.
  - Border: 2px solid `#FFC72C`.
  - Bevel: `0 4px 0 #120907`. Text in `#FFC72C`.
- **Tertiary ("RETRY WITH DURVA BOOST")**:
  - Surface: Emerald-to-Durva green gradient (`#4CAF50` to `#2E7D32`), with deep forest bevel `0 5px 0 #1B4D20`.

### 2. Modak & Coin HUD Tickers
- Pill container with midnight brown base (`#1A0D08`) and a 1.5px gold border (`#FFC72C`).
- Left-anchored circular gold coin or modak icon overlapping the pill boundary by -8px with specular gold halo.
- Numeric typography in Epilogue 800 (`counter-lg`), colored in `#FFF8E7` with golden thousand-separators.
- Plus-button CTA nested at the right edge for instant temple store access.

### 3. Dialog Modals & Brass Filigree Panels
- Base canvas `#23120B` with 94% opacity.
- Perimeter: Double-line brass border (1.5px outer gold `#FFE082`, 1px inner amber `#FF7A00` with 3px separation).
- Corner Flourishes: Symmetrical 16x16px brass filigree brackets at all four corners.
- Header Banner: Arched banner ribbon dropping from top edge with rich vermilion fill and golden ribbon ends.

### 4. Combo & Streak Badges
- Shape: Starburst scalloped polygon inspired by traditional rangoli chakras.
- Background: Glowing radiant saffron (`#FF7A00`) with pulsating golden border.
- Text: "x5 COMBO" or "MODAK FRENZY" set in `display-hero-mobile`, rotated -4 degrees to convey kinetic arcade speed.
- Dynamic burst effect: Ring particle emitter radiating outwards when combo increments.

### 5. Cards & Character / Vehicle Loadout Slots
- Card Body: `#1A0D08` base, `rounded-lg` (16px), with padded display platform.
- Unlocked State: Radiant soft golden floor spotlight (`#FFC72C` at 20% opacity) beneath Mushak sprite.
- Locked State: 50% opacity wash, dark brass padlock glyph, cost badge highlighted in `#FFC72C`.
- Equipped Chip: Small durva-green pill (`#2E7D32`) floating at bottom-center with text "EQUIPPED" in `#FFF8E7`.

### 6. Interactive Touch Arcade Controls
- Floating or fixed thumb-stick and lane-switch tap targets.
- Semi-transparent brass ring outline (`rgba(255, 199, 44, 0.3)`) that lights up to 100% solid saffron on finger contact.
- Tactile feedback: Visual shockwave ripple originating from touch coordinates.

### 7. Durva Boost Progress Meter
- Container: Rounded pill with recessed dark well (`#120907`) and gold border.
- Fill Track: High-energy gradient from `#2E7D32` to `#4CAF50` with animated diagonal candy-cane speed stripes.
- Particle Cap: Glowing leaf ember at the leading edge of the progress fill indicating ready state.
